## Named box

Venturi Astrolab **FLIP** (FLEX Lunar Innovation Platform) on Astrobotic **Griffin-1**, Mons Mouton.

Night Line record: [LINES.md](LINES.md). Identity: `usable_Wh / 354 h = hibernation load W`. Store Wh and hibernation load W are both OPEN. An ASSUMED 30 % table is not a picked store. This clock does not close that line.

2. Astrolab home heading: “Power to Survive the Lunar Night.” Body: FLIP’s collapsible solar array charges a Venturi battery; “the battery on FLIP is the same full-size battery found on FLEX.” Watt-hours are not printed.  
   https://www.astrolab.space/

3. LPSC 2026 1874, Operational Concept: “Astrolab is developing FLIP's operational concept for flexibility in landing times, communications availability, and lunar hibernation.” The paper prints launch mass 480 kg as the VIPER geometry and launch-mass envelope, not a battery number.  
   https://www.hou.usra.edu/meetings/lpsc2026/pdf/1874.pdf

This clock tests those **hibernation / survive-the-night sentences**. It does not mint LIVE or DIE from missing watt-hours.

## When the clock is due

**Witness event:** the first sunset after Griffin-1 landing, then whether FLIP is publicly reported awake, in hibernation, dead, or silent.

**Landing date:** OPEN until a printed landing, or a printed surface-ops start.

**Launch NET is not that date.** Aerospace America (15 September 2026) prints that Griffin is on track to launch **no earlier than November 2026**, and that the lander is to touch down at Mons Mouton. NET November 2026 is a no-earlier-than **launch** print. It is not a landing date. It is not a sunset date. This page does not compute a Mons Mouton sunset calendar from NET.

Other page prints still on the web do not close landing either:

- FLIP page: “scheduled to land at the Lunar South Pole in Summer 2026”
- LPSC 1874: “scheduled to launch in mid-2026” (older than the November NET)
- Home: “FLIP will travel to the Moon in 2026”

**Night length on this clock:** not Cataldo & Mason 354 h, unless a page prints that night for Mons Mouton / this flight. 354 h stays the darkness *reference* on the Night Line identity, not a sunset timer.

**Status on 2026-09-17:** Griffin-1 has not landed. Clock pending.

## What would close the Night Line (not this clock)

Printed **store Wh** or printed average **hibernation load W**, with page or figure. Until one of those is printed, the line stays `LINE_OPEN_TWO_KNOBS`. Do not inherit a VIPER battery number; LPSC 1874 quotes launch-mass and geometry only.
