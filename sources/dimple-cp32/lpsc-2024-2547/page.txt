IMPLEMENTING LUNAR IN SITU DATING WITH THE DIMPLE PAYLOAD.
F. S. Anderson, Southwest Research Institute, Boulder, CO 80302 USA.

In the enhanced mission scenario, the payload analyzes over six samples from various locations on and around Mons Agnes, in a single lunar day.

All surface operations are to be completed within a single lunar day of ~348 hours, of which 8 are budgeted for instrument commissioning.

This work is supported through the NASA PRISM grant 80NSSC24M0001.
