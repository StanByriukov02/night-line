Threshold Services
Operate the instrument suite for two lunar days, including survival through one lunar night
There will be no night operations supported in the threshold mission
Propose science objectives that can be accomplished within this threshold mission duration

Goal Services (not guaranteed)
Operate through up to six lunar days, including survival through up to five lunar nights
Payload suites must remain within night keep-alive power restrictions, including peak and sustained electric and thermal power draws
No data storage or communications services will be provided at night
