Rigorous selection, critical testing
Cell after cell

Each cell is analysed and subjected to a series of tests, from delivery to final installation in the battery pack. Of the 10,000 units delivered to the Monaco laboratory, only 80% will be selected.
The cells are subjected to a sequence of performance and destructive testing:

Step 1 : Dimensions Mass | Length | Diameter
Step 2 : Performance Charge | Discharge | Internal resistance
Step 3 : Endurance Simulations over lunar cycles under vacuum conditions and at a range of temperatures storage
Step 4 : Safety External short circuit | Internal short circuit | Overload | Over-discharge | Vibrations | Shocks | Impacts | Vacuum resistance | Radiations resistance
Battery pack The battery pack (i.e. cell assembly) is put through the same tests as the cells, but also needs to be resistant to thermal runaway and the potential leakage of active components at 1,600°C.
