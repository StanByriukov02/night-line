## 1. Cited procurement (opened 2026-09-17)

NASA JSC [CLPS 2.0 procurement](https://www.nasa.gov/johnson/jsc-procurement/clps2/), table updated 2026-09-14: contract award planned **2026-09-22**; contract start **2026-10-01**. As of 17 September 2026 the award list on that page was blank. I do not invent an award.

[CLPS 1.0](https://www.nasa.gov/commercial-lunar-payload-services/): NASA prints indefinite-delivery, indefinite-quantity contracts with a combined maximum contract value of **$2.6 billion through November 2028**. That is NASA’s published IDIQ figure, not a price on this reconstruction.

NASA [CS-8](https://www.nasa.gov/ignition/) prints a performance bonus for deploying a radioisotope device and transmitting from the lunar surface after a full lunar night. That bonus is a different NASA-paid object from this appendix.

## 2. What is purchased

Independent reconstruction of a Night Line on the named payload from (a) already-published numbers and/or (b) one team-provided number with a page or figure.

Deliverable:

- `LINE.md` — the line, the label, the OPEN knobs, and the cites
- `INPUTS.csv` — every input marked `cited`, `OPEN`, or `team_provided`
- a dated public row, if the buyer allows it; otherwise a private `LINE.md` only

A Night Line is the largest average electrical load the named box can carry through the printed night on the team’s own printed store, reserves, and efficiencies. Unprinted knobs stay OPEN. The label is on declared corners only; it is not a claim that the box will live.

- Measurement-lab coupons, or any measurement by me
- A C-corporation invoice — the buyer pays Stanislav Byriukov as an individual
